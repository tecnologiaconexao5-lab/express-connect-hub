## Causa do Gap

ENV-QA-08A's `m08a_deploy.sql` referenced `portal_ocorrencias.os_id` in `po_select_tms` and `po_update_tms` policies. This column does not exist, causing:
- 1st apply: policies created but with errors
- 2nd+ apply: DROP IF EXISTS removes policies, CREATE fails, policies lost

Additionally, `tenant_usuarios` had RLS enabled but **zero policies**, causing `EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM tenant_usuarios WHERE user_id = auth.uid())` to return false for non-superuser roles.

## Solução Aplicada

1. **portal_ocorrencias policies** use `cliente_id = f_obter_cliente_id_do_usuario()` instead of `os_id`
2. **tenant_usuarios** gets `tu_select_own` policy (SELECT WHERE user_id = auth.uid())
3. **pp_ocorrencias_all** (USING(true)) explicitly dropped

## Policies Finais — portal_ocorrencias
